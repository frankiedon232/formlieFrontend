# Design references

The owner's ideas for the website, from 2026-10-10. They are **ideas for layout and feel**: they don't replace the app's look, and their brands, wording and colours are never copied. What to take from each is in `docs/DESIGN.md`.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `references/01-shopwave-landing.png` | A SaaS landing page: split hero with floating product cards and stat chips, logo strip, "What sets us apart" cards |
| `references/02-deflexai-sections.webp` | A sheet of sections: centred hero with logos on orbit rings, feature and notification cards, integrations list, a footer |
| `references/03-codivoo-products.webp` | A product page: hero with stat tiles, product tabs with a large feature card, a features grid with mini charts |

The Formalie look itself (fonts, ink, radius, shadows, animations) comes from the app: see `docs/reference/` and `docs/screenshots/`.
