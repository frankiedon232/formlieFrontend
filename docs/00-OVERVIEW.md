# 00, Overview

## Positioning (owner)

**Formalie is a secure, flexible platform for creating forms, collecting and managing data, connecting systems and controlling access, built for organisations of any size, anywhere in the world.**

Organisations can:
- build any form and design it as they want;
- share it as a link, embed it on their website or open it to their apps through an API;
- keep the answers in Formalie's encrypted storage or their **own database**;
- decide exactly who sees what;
- stay privacy-compliant.

## Who it is for

| Audience | What they need from the site |
|---|---|
| Small teams and individuals | Start free (Starter), see it is simple, sign up in a minute |
| Growing companies | Their own domain and sending address, company sign-in, more forms: Professional |
| Established companies with heavy data | No limits, every database, fast extraction: Business |
| Large organisations, regulated sectors | Security, data residency, dedicated support, contracts: Enterprise, "Contact sales" |
| Developers | The API service, endpoints, tokens, webhooks, docs |
| Data teams | Bring your own database (MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Oracle), explorer, query editor |

## Tone

- Clear, calm and confident, in plain words, with no jargon unless a developer section needs it.
- Short sentences. Benefits first, then the feature that delivers them.
- International and neutral: examples from many countries, no single culture.
- Compliance: promise **controls** ("privacy and compliance controls"), never certifications Formalie does not hold.
- No em dashes.

## Goals of the website

1. Explain what Formalie does in five seconds, through the hero, the data-flow animation and three proof points.
2. Show the product honestly, with real screens (cropped, watermarked).
3. Turn visits into sign-ups (Starter, free) and sales enquiries (Enterprise).
4. Answer the questions people ask before buying: pricing, security, data location, integrations, languages and support.
5. Host the legal pages.

## Key facts to use (true today)

- **20 languages** in the app and on forms: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic (right to left), Hindi, Bengali, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Korean, Indonesian, Vietnamese and Swahili.
- **Answers from everywhere:** form link, website embed, QR code and API, all landing in one place.
- **Your database or ours:** MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, SQL Server or Oracle, or Formalie's encrypted storage.
- **Access control:** roles with permissions, folder access, per-form sharing, and "who can see this form".
- **Security:**
  - encrypted in transit and at rest, with end-to-end encrypted requests between the app and the server;
  - two-step sign-in and single sign-on (Okta and others);
  - an audit trail of every action;
  - data residency (Enterprise).
- **AI assistant:** builds forms from a description or a document, analyses responses and translates.
- **Help centre:** articles in 20 languages, guided tours on every page and an assistant.
